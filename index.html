About

Where admin work meets software

I'm a BSIT graduate from CBSUA-Sipocot who found my footing at the intersection of government service and software — encoding public health data by day, building small systems that make that work faster whenever I get the chance.

My capstone, E-SWKS, was a web-based information management system built for my university's language and culture center — my first real experience turning a messy manual process into something structured and usable.

Since then I've kept building: a scheduling app for our church's music ministry, exam reviewers for LTO applicants, a document automation concept called DocuWork PH, and the everyday systems that keep our rural health unit running.
